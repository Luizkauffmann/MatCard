Privacy Policy

Mat Cards

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Mat Cards (the "App"), developed by Luiz Kauffmann ("I", "me"), handles your information. It applies to the App on Android and iOS.

Summary

Mat Cards is a single-player game. It does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information, does not show ads, does not include analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell or share personal data with anyone.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (belt rank, unlocked cards, decks and settings) is saved only on your device. I cannot see or access it. Deleting the App removes this data.

Game content updates

When you open the App with an internet connection, it may download updated game content (card data and artwork) from a content delivery service. Like any website, that service receives standard technical information needed to deliver the files, such as your IP address and the time of the request, and may keep it in short-term server logs for security and reliability. This information is not linked to you, is not used to identify you, and is not used for advertising. The App works fully without an internet connection.

Information I do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or contacts
  • No location data
  • No photos, files, camera or microphone access
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, usage tracking or crash reporting by the App itself
  • No payment information (the App has no in-app purchases)

The app stores (Google Play and the Apple App Store) may provide me with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as download counts and crash reports. These are governed by Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing of information

I do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties, because the App does not collect any.

Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If future versions of the App add features such as accounts, cloud saves or online play, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
